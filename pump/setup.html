Wire and upload once

Use a normally-open momentary button. One wire goes to D2, the other to GND. Either way around works. No power wire or extra resistor is needed.

  1. Switch off the motor’s supply and connect the UNO R4 WiFi to your computer by USB.
  2. Download and unzip the firmware. Open pump_ble.ino in Arduino IDE; keep the two headers in the same folder.
  3. Install Arduino UNO R4 Boards and the ArduinoBLE library. Choose Arduino UNO R4 WiFi, select its port, and upload.
  4. Power the motor again. Open Button settings, connect to Eric Pump, enable the button, enter mL per press, and save.

Each press delivers one timed dose. Release before pressing again. The dose stays saved when power is removed, and the physical button works without Bluetooth.

The existing 90 mL/min conversion is an estimate. Measure collected liquid to verify actual output. 0.3 mL ≈ 0.2 s; 1.5 mL ≈ 1 s; 3 mL ≈ 2 s.
